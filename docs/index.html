Questions

Before you order

How do I place an order?

Tap any “Order on WhatsApp” button. A chat opens with your chosen perfume and size. Send the message and we will reply to confirm the price, availability and delivery.

Why are prices not listed?

Prices of designer fragrances change often. We confirm the current price for your size in the chat, so you always get an up-to-date quote.

I don’t know which perfume to choose.

Tell us what you like (fresh, sweet, woody, spicy) or a scent you already love, and we will suggest options that suit you or the person you are buying for.

Can I request a perfume that isn’t listed?

Yes. Send the name on WhatsApp and we will check whether we can source it for you.
