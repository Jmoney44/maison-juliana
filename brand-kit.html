Chapter I

Brand essence

Who Maison Juliana is, what it stands for, and how it should feel at every touchpoint, from a bottle on a vanity to a caption on Instagram.

A house of fragrance for people who treat scent as a signature, not an accessory. Every bottle is chosen to linger, to be remembered, and to feel like an occasion.

Mission

Make true luxury fragrance personal and reachable, curated with care and presented like a gift every time.

Promise

Luxury in every scent. Long-lasting oils and parfums, honest notes, and packaging worth keeping.

Audience

Confident women and men, 22–45, who dress with intention, gift generously and want to be remembered after they leave the room.

Chapter II

Name architecture

The four names work as one family. Maison Juliana is the house; the others each have a clear job so the brand never feels scattered.

Maison Juliana
Master brand

The house name. Use on the logo, packaging, website, storefront, invoices and anything formal or legal.

Juliana Luxe
Premium collection

The top tier: extrait de parfum, limited editions and gift boxes. Always written as “Juliana Luxe by Maison Juliana” on first mention.

Juli Scent
Everyday line · nickname

The friendly short form for body mists, travel sizes, oils and casual social content. Never used on the premium range.

Juliana Scent
Descriptor only

A search and conversation phrase, e.g. “shop Juliana scents”. Not a logo and never set in the wordmark style.

Chapter IV

Colour

Onyx and gold carry the brand. Ivory gives it breathing room on print and packaging. Keep the palette disciplined: a page should read as black and gold first.

Chapter VI

Frames & ornament

The double hairline frame from the original logo card is the brand's signature graphic. Use it to hold content, never to fill space.

The double frame
New Arrival

Two 1px gold lines, 7px apart, with squared corner accents. Line opacity 30–40%.

Fan pattern

Art Deco fan for tissue paper, box liners, ribbon and story backgrounds.

Pinstripe & divider

Pinstripe for box sides. Diamond divider between sections or under headlines.

Photography
  • Low-key lighting: dark backgrounds, one warm light source, deep shadows.
  • Glass, gold, black marble, silk, dried florals, candlelight.
  • Close crops of hands, wrists and necks applying scent.
  • Warm grade; lift golds, keep blacks rich.
Avoid
  • Bright white studio backgrounds or neon colours.
  • Stock-looking smiles and cluttered flat-lays.
  • Heavy filters, stickers and more than one emoji per caption.
  • Pricing splashes like “SALE!!!” over product shots.
Chapter VII

Voice & tone

Maison Juliana speaks like a trusted perfumer: warm, assured and sensory. It describes how a scent feels and when to wear it. It never shouts.

Sensory

Describe notes, texture and mood. “Soft amber that settles on warm skin.”

Assured

Short, confident sentences. No pleading, no countdown pressure.

Personal

Speak to one person. “Your signature” rather than “our customers”.
